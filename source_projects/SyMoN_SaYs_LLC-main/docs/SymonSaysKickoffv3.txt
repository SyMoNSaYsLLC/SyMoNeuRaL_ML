11. Under the hood, SyMoNeuRaL leverages the most powerful tools in the open-source ecosystem
    a. Python Builders: We use custom Python scripts to automate layer management and configuration injection.
    b. AUTOREV & Continuous Integration: By utilizing AUTOREV, our system automatically pulls the latest revisions from our git repositories, ensuring we are always building on the "bleeding edge" of our development.
    c. RunQEMU: We use sophisticated hardware emulation to test our builds in a virtual environment before they are ever flashed to physical silicon, reducing the risk of "bricking" hardware.
    d. Autotools: For standardized software compilation and cross-platform compatibility.
